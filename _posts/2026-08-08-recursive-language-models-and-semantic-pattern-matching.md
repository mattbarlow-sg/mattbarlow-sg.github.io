At the end of 2025, Alex L. Zhang, Tim Kraska, and Omar Khattab published a paper, [*Recursive Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24601).

Since then, I've been experimenting with it for the use case of semantic pattern matching in deeply nested, multi-schema data sources.

I was excited to find [*Recursive Agent Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06639) yesterday, which demonstrates this same pattern on models trained to work with the RLM scaffold.
